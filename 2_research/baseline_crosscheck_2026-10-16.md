# Cross check of the 2021 baseline against Leliveld and Provolo 2020

Run on 16 October 2026, as the literature check of 29 September 2026 planned. Leliveld and Provolo 2020 (Animals 10:1430, [leliveld_provolo_2020.xml](../1_sources/leliveld_provolo_2020.xml), with its supplementary Tables S1 to S3 in [leliveld_provolo_2020_supplementary.zip](../1_sources/leliveld_provolo_2020_supplementary.zip)) is a review of welfare indicators of indoor housed dairy cows that could be monitored automatically. It is from 2020, so it is a second source for the same baseline as Maroto Molina 2020, not evidence since 2021. It changes no grade; it records whether its account agrees with each grade that rests on Maroto Molina: research_demonstrated, theoretically_measurable and none.

Result: of 43 rows, 20 agree, 23 are not covered by the review, and 0 disagree. Only a few rows are positively confirmed: I011 (the review cites the same agonistic behaviour study as Stygar, Foris et al. 2019), I048 (like Maroto Molina, it leaves mortality out because automated systems should prevent it rather than measure it) and the water point rows I030, I032 and I033 (it names RumiWatch for water intake, the same substitute measure). The other rows agree only because the review is silent, and the review says that evaluating detection techniques is not its focus, so its silence is weak evidence. It also leaves out by design pasture held cattle, measures that follow from farmer decisions, mortality and reproductive performance, and measures that need a physical examination.

Two rows to watch: the review names self grooming (I017) and difficulty getting up or down (I015, related to the getting up movement) as indicators that could be measured automatically, but names no technology, so under rule 4.2 they stay none in the 2021 baseline.

The check was done by an AI agent from fixed instructions, and each quote was confirmed by script to appear word for word in the source.
