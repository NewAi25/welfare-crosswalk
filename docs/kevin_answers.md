## Decisions taken from this, 25 September 2026

1. Species: dairy first, then a poultry unit (broilers, or laying hens if the sensor and indicator sources turn out better). The poultry unit is a commitment, not an option.
2. The three criteria are adoption factors, not filters. The deliverable is a ranked table of indicators by ease of adoption, with every factor shown as its own sourced column and no factor used to exclude a row. The two valuable cell lists from item #13 are kept as views of that table.
3. Industry acceptability is read as "how easily can this be implemented, at low or no cost". FARM v5 naming stays as one visible factor. Two further sourced factors are added so the reading does not depend on judgement: the hardware class the measurement needs (routine data already collected, a fixed device per farm, a device per animal, or a sample or procedure), and market breadth, the number of commercial products in the Stygar appendix that sell the measurement, on the reasoning that what many vendors already sell is what industry already accepts.
4. Certification coverage stays a factor: number of the four schemes that require the indicator, with the audit method, not a gate.
5. No further questions go to Kevin. Any refinement of these factors is a written rule in standard/definitions.md with a decisions entry.
